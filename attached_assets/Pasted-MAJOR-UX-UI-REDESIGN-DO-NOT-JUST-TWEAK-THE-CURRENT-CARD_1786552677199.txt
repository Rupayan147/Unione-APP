MAJOR UX/UI REDESIGN — DO NOT JUST TWEAK THE CURRENT CARDS.

I reviewed the current app. The biggest problem is NOT functionality. The app feels like disconnected cards stacked on a screen. It has no visual identity, no emotional journey, no motivation, and too much visual clutter.

I want you to redesign the EXPERIENCE, especially HOME, around one clear idea:

"Unione helps you move from uncertainty → discovery → understanding → action."

Do NOT simply rearrange the existing components. Recompose the screens.

==================================================
1. NEW VISUAL DIRECTION
==================================================

Make Unione feel like a premium, optimistic, human-centered civic-tech app.

Think:

Apple-level simplicity
+ modern fintech polish
+ Duolingo-like motivation
+ Calm-like emotional warmth
+ premium AI product

The app should feel:
- hopeful
- positive
- intelligent
- trustworthy
- encouraging
- clean
- modern

Use a WHITE / very light background.

Color system:
- deep navy for text
- vibrant teal as primary brand color
- fresh turquoise
- soft lavender
- subtle sky blue
- occasional warm accent

Use color to create hierarchy, not decoration.

NO dark teal backgrounds.

NO huge number of bordered cards.

NO excessive pills.

NO random colors for every section.

NO visual clutter.

==================================================
2. CREATE A REAL UNIONE BRAND IDENTITY
==================================================

The current UI looks like a generic template.

Create a stronger visual identity.

The Unione brand should have:
- distinctive U-inspired visual language
- subtle flowing/connecting shapes
- small abstract illustrations
- friendly AI visual
- consistent icon style
- consistent accent colors

Use visual elements to communicate:

"Connecting people with support."

Do not make it childish.

==================================================
3. HOME SCREEN MUST BECOME A JOURNEY
==================================================

The Home screen should NOT feel like:

Greeting
↓
Profile card
↓
3 cards
↓
3 more cards
↓
more cards

Instead, create a narrative.

Top:

UNIONE

"Good morning, Sarah 👋"

"Let's find the support that's right for you."

Then create a visually beautiful HERO SECTION:

"Your path to support"

Show a visual journey:

DISCOVER → UNDERSTAND → APPLY → TRACK

Use a connected flowing line/path rather than four unrelated cards.

Current stage:

01 DISCOVER

"You're already on your way."

Then a strong CTA:

"Continue your journey →"

This should take the user to Discover.

==================================================
4. MOTIVATE THE USER
==================================================

Unione should make the user feel progress.

Create a section such as:

"You're 1 step closer"

or

"Let's take the next step together."

Example:

✓ Tell us about you
✓ Find potential benefits
→ Understand your best matches
○ Apply
○ Track your progress

Use visual progress.

The user should understand:

"I don't need to figure everything out myself. Unione will guide me."

This is CRITICAL to the product.

==================================================

Ask Unione is the MAIN USP.

The experience should feel like:

"Talk to someone who helps you understand your options."

Top:

"Not sure where to start?"

"Tell me what's going on. I'll help you find your next step."

Create a beautiful AI visual/character/abstract Unione assistant.

Then a prominent input:

"Tell Unione what you need help with..."

Below:

"Try asking"

"What support could I get?"

"I recently lost my job."

"Help with rent"

"What should I do next?"

The AI conversation should visually guide the user toward ACTION.

After an answer, show:

YOUR NEXT STEP

"Explore SNAP"

"See what you'll need"

rather than just a long chatbot response.
